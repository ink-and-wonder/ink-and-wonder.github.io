# Art production notes

All three illustrated environments were generated with the built-in image generation tool. The runtime game uses WebP copies; original PNGs remain in the local `art-source` folder and the generation library. The exported game does not depend on those source PNGs. The lantern, plant, paper bird, kettle, mill, oven, gate, basin, celestial instrument, UI symbols, and app icon are code-native SVG/vector artwork.

## Atelier

Runtime asset: `dist/assets/atelier.webp`. Original local asset: `art-source/atelier.png`.

Prompt:

> Create a premium fantasy manga background illustration for a Witch Hat Atelier inspired drawing game, no text or typography. Wide landscape composition. An intricately inked medieval witch's atelier with tall arched windows, shelves of scrolls and glass ink bottles, hanging herbs, an old wooden desk in foreground holding open books and a small unlit brass lantern, distant rolling green hills through window. On right side a small white haired young apprentice in an oversized ivory conical witch hat and dark cloak seen from behind reading a book. Refined fine black pen crosshatching, delicate watercolor washes of muted sage green, ivory parchment, warm gold, richly detailed European storybook architecture, atmospheric afternoon sunlight, sophisticated manga panel illustration, beautifully composed, no modern objects, no interface. 1536x1024 landscape.

## Village

Runtime asset: `dist/assets/village.webp`. Original local asset: `art-source/village.png`.

Prompt:

> Original premium fantasy manga environment illustration for a full-screen tablet adventure game inspired by the world of Witch Hat Atelier. No words, UI, lettering, or logos. Wide 1536x1024 landscape. A serene medieval hillside village seen from a cobblestone footbridge, a small water channel winding through the lower center, old stone cottages with steep slate roofs, linen banners and wildflowers, an ancient circular arch on left, a tall wooden windmill on the upper right hillside, a distant forest and mountain range. Tiny young witch apprentice with ivory pointed hat and dark blue cape seen from behind on bridge at lower left. Elegant fine black ink crosshatching with delicate watercolor sage greens, smoky blue, warm ivory, muted gold. Immaculate detailed fantasy architectural drawing, a tangible storybook world with strong depth, golden afternoon sun and atmospheric haze. Clear central lower foreground for game object overlays, landscape painting fills frame.
